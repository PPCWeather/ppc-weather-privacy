PPC Weather — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

PPC Weather is a free, ad-free Android app that helps powered parachute pilots review weather forecasts and aviation reference information. This policy explains what information the app uses, how it is stored, and what may be transmitted to external information providers. For privacy questions, contact help@chuteflyers.com.

1. Information you provide or allow the app to access

Selected flying locations. You can select an airport, search for a city or ZIP code, enter coordinates, or save a custom flying location. The app uses the selected location to retrieve applicable forecasts and aviation information.

Optional device location. If you choose “Current Location,” PPC Weather requests Android location permission to determine your device’s latitude and longitude. Device location is not needed when you use a manually entered or selected location. The app does not request background-location permission. You can deny or revoke location access through Android Settings.

Saved preferences. Saved or favorite locations, coordinates, display units, wind thresholds, and other settings are stored locally on your device.

2. Information transmitted to external services

PPC Weather uses third-party services to retrieve forecasts and reference information. Depending on the feature you use, requests may contain location coordinates, an airport identifier, a town name, or a ZIP code. External services also receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, and may keep their own access logs.

  • Open-Meteo: Receives coordinates of the location selected for weather forecasts. Its geocoding service may receive town or place search text.
  • Zippopotam.us: May receive a ZIP code you enter to identify a location.
  • Android location and geocoding services: Device location is obtained through Android's location capabilities. When available, Android's geocoder may process selected GPS coordinates to identify a nearby town; processing may depend on the services configured on your device.
  • NOAA Aviation Weather Center: May receive an airport identifier when PPC Weather retrieves METAR/TAF aviation weather reports.
  • GitHub-hosted FAA data and OurAirports datasets: Supply airport, runway, frequency, airspace, and related reference information. Network requests to these hosts may expose standard connection information.

Each provider operates under its own privacy practices and data-retention policies. PPC Weather does not control the providers' server logs. Optional links that open outside websites are governed by those websites' policies.

3. Advertising, analytics, and accounts

PPC Weather does not require a user account or sign-in. The current app does not contain advertising or an advertising SDK, and it does not include a developer-operated analytics or user-tracking service. The developer does not sell user information.

4. Local storage, retention, and deletion

Saved locations, coordinates, and preferences are kept in app-local storage on your device. Downloaded FAA reference datasets and temporary weather information may also be stored on the device to support app functionality. Local information remains until you change or remove it, clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling normally removes the app's locally stored information.

Information transmitted in requests to external providers is subject to those providers' separate retention practices; clearing local app data cannot erase their server logs. Because PPC Weather does not operate user accounts or a separate user-information database, there is no developer-held account profile to delete.

5. Security

PPC Weather uses HTTPS for external forecast and dataset downloads. Its Android app also uses a device-local loopback connection to display the bundled user interface; this is not a public web server. No internet-based system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Children

PPC Weather is designed as a weather and aviation planning utility and is not specifically directed to children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when application functionality, information providers, or applicable requirements change. Revisions will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact

For support or privacy-related questions about PPC Weather, email help@chuteflyers.com.
